HW3 / Lecture homework

Designing for augmented reality

Not started · assignment outline only

The sections below follow HW3.pdf. They describe the required work; they are not completed answers.

Lecture 7 · Ideate · 5 points

Create, sketch and present at least three ideas for enhancing human abilities with augmented reality.

Evidence to prepare: Three labelled concept sketches, the intended users and the abilities each idea supports.

Lecture 8 · Prototype · 5 points

Build a prototype that is ready for a first user exposure, such as a paper prototype or Wizard-of-Oz prototype.

Evidence to prepare: Photos or video, interaction steps and an explanation of what the prototype represents.

Lecture 9 · Evaluate · 5 points

Ask at least one person other than yourself to interact with the prototype. Observe, discuss their opinion and reflect critically.

Evidence to prepare: Actual observations, feedback, limitations and proposed changes. No invented participant results.
