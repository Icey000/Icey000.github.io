01 / French in context

A little help,
right where you look.

Understand a French notice without losing its context. This is a clickable simulation with a fictional illustration and fixed translations. It does not use your camera or upload data.

Illustrated scene / library entrance

BIBLIOTHÈQUE

Select a line to translate. The original notice stays visible.

What is simulated?

Selecting a line stands in for pointing at text in AR. The result is predefined. “Unclear text” deliberately simulates recognition failure; it does not measure image quality.

First user exposure

Ask another person to try a task before explaining the controls. Record their actual actions and comments in the blank observation sheet. No test results have been recorded yet.
